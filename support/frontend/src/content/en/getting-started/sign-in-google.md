title: "Sign in with Google"
category: getting-started
slug: sign-in-google
audience: all
order: 4
summary: "Use Google Sign-In when the button is shown on the login screen."
---

On the sign-in screen, choose **Continue with Google** and pick the Google account that matches your WebOnOne email.

If Google Sign-In is not shown, your site has not enabled it — use [email and password](/docs/getting-started/sign-in) instead.

After Google finishes, you return to the app and [choose an account](/docs/getting-started/choose-account) if needed.
