title: "Create an account"
category: getting-started
slug: create-account
audience: all
order: 2
summary: "Register with your name, email, and password from the WebOnOne app."
---

1. Open the WebOnOne app and choose **Register** (or open Sign in, then the register link).
2. Enter your name, email, and a password.
3. Submit the form. You are taken to sign in if you are not signed in automatically.

Use the email you can receive messages on. You can verify email and phone later from your [profile](/docs/people/edit-profile).

Already have an account? [Sign in](/docs/getting-started/sign-in) instead.
