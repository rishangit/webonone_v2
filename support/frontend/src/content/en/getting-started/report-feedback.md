Customers can tell the WebOnOne team about something that broke or a feature they would like added. Reports are saved on this help site so everyone signed in can see what was submitted.

## Open the report list

Use any of these:

1. At the bottom of the left menu, choose **[Bug & feature reports](/feedback)**.
2. On the [help home page](/), scroll to **Browse topics** and choose **Open bug and feature log**.
3. Go directly to [Bug & feature reports](/feedback).

If you are not signed in, choose **Sign in** first (same WebOnOne email and password as the app). You return to the report list after login.

## Submit a report

1. On the [report list](/feedback), choose **Report issue**.
2. Pick **Bug** or **Feature request**.
3. Enter a short **title** and a **description** (steps to reproduce, expected behavior, or what you want added).
4. Choose **Submit report**.

Your report appears in the shared list for everyone who is signed in.

## View reports

All signed-in users see the same list of reports, with type, status, who submitted it, and when.

Use **Search** to find text in titles or descriptions. Use the filter panel to narrow by type or status.

## Status (Super Admin)

Only a **Super Admin** can change report status:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **To do** | Received, not started |
| **In progress** | Being worked on |
| **Completed** | Done or shipped |

Open the three-dot menu on a row (Super Admin only) and pick the new status.
