The desktop app is WebOnOne in a window on your computer. Everything you can do in the browser — calendar, people, catalog, messages, sales, and your website — is available here.

## Install

1. In the app, open **Settings → Basic Settings → Downloads**.
2. Choose **Download for Windows** to save `WebOnOne-Setup.exe`.
3. Run the installer and follow the prompts.
4. Open **WebOnOne** from the Start menu or desktop shortcut.

## Sign in

Sign in the same way you do in the browser: [email and password](/docs/getting-started/sign-in) or [Google](/docs/getting-started/sign-in-google) when that option is shown.

Your sign-in stays after you close the window. You still need an internet connection.

## Features stay in sync with the website

You do **not** need to reinstall when we add pages or change the app. After we update the website, open the desktop app again (or refresh) to see the new features.

Reinstall only if your team sends a new installer for the desktop window itself.

## Links and files

- Help and other websites open in your usual browser, not inside the desktop app.
- Downloads go to your computer’s Downloads folder.

If the app cannot reach WebOnOne, check your internet connection and choose **Retry**.
