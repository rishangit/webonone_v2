WebOnOne shows different left-nav items depending on the account you chose.

- **Member (Default User)** — register companies, connect to companies, use calendar as a guest of connected companies, and edit your profile and settings.
- **Company owner** — full company menu: calendar, people, catalog, email, SMS, sales, invoices, design, and company profile.
- **Staff** — front-desk work: schedule, sessions, and related company tools your owner enabled.
- **Super Admin** — platform-wide companies, catalog library verification, email/SMS at platform scope, and invoices. Super Admin does not use a single company’s calendar or POS.

See [Choose your account](/docs/getting-started/choose-account) to pick a role after login.
