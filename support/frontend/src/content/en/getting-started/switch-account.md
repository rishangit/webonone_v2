You can change account without signing out.

**From settings**

1. Open **Settings → Basic Settings**.
2. Open the **Account** section.
3. Choose the member, company, staff, or Super Admin account you need.

**From the header**

Use **Change account** when it appears in the user menu, then pick the same options.

Company accounts only appear after the company is [approved](/docs/companies/pending-approval).
