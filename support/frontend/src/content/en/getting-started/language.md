category: getting-started

summary: "Switch the app between English and Sinhala from the header language menu."
---

1. In the header, open the **language** (globe) menu.
2. Choose **English** or **සිංහල**.

The choice is remembered for this browser. When you are signed in, it can also be saved on your profile.
