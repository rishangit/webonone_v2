---

1. Open the public website (webonone.com).
2. The home page puts search at the top. Brand headline text is not shown there.
3. Below search, nearby products, services, and spaces appear as cards, closest first. On a phone they span the same width as the header and show two cards in each row. Open a card to see the offering.
4. Type a name, description, or tag and choose **Search** to open the full search results.

You do not need an account to browse. Nearby cards use your location (or an approximate location if precise location is off). Booking a token may ask you to [sign in](/docs/public-catalog/website-sign-in).
