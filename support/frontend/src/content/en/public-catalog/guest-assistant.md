On webonone.com, use the **chat** button (usually bottom-right or in the header).

Guests can ask about search and offerings. Signed-in users get a richer assistant when their [AI key](/docs/app-preferences/ai-api-key) is set. Super Admins configure the guest provider on the platform AI card.
