Signed-in AI lives in the WebOnOne assistant and the AI service app.

- In the app: [AI assistant](/docs/app-preferences/ai-assistant)
- Conversations can use tools from Data and other services; **confirm** when the assistant wants to create or change records.

The assistant never replaces Calendar or POS for front-desk work — it helps you get there faster.
