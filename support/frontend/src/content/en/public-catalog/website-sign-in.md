summary: "Use Login on webonone.com, then optionally Open app to continue in WebOnOne."
---

1. On the public site, choose **Login**.
2. Sign in with Identity (same account as the app).
3. After login, **Open app** takes you into WebOnOne with your session.

Browse and search stay available without signing in.
