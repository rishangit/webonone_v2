summary: "Design fields and layout in the Design form designer."
---

1. Open a form from **Design → Forms**.
2. Add a field from the toolbox. It appears on the canvas with **Edit** in the top-right corner.
3. Tap or click **Edit** to open the field properties panel on the right. In WebOnOne it slides over the **full shell** (sidebar and header included), like list filters. On standalone Design it slides over the page the same way. Change the label, required setting, and options there.
4. Close the panel, add more fields as needed, then **Save**.

Staff can [fill the form in a session](/docs/calendar/session-forms) without opening this designer.
