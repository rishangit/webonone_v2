summary: "Add a Nav menu add-on to a header. Links come from the pages on that layout. On phones the menu becomes a hamburger and a slide-over."
---

1. Open a **header** in the designer.
2. Add a content element, then add the **Nav menu** add-on (headers only).
3. Place it where the links should sit (usually beside the logo). Optionally pick a text style and alignment.
4. Assign pages to the layout that uses this header. Those **active** pages are the menu, in the layout’s menu order.

On **wide screens**, the add-on shows a row of page names. On **phones** (the `sm` canvas), the same add-on shows a **menu** icon. Tap it for a panel from the right with the same links. Tap a page to go there; tap outside, **X**, or Escape to close.

Do not use Button add-ons for the main nav. Buttons are for calls to action and do not collapse on mobile.

See [Website layouts](/docs/website-forms/website-layouts) and [Headers and footers](/docs/website-forms/headers-footers).
