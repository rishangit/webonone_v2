---

1. Open **Design → Website** (pages).
2. Add a page or open an existing one.
3. Use the page designer to add content, then save.
4. Open the live site from the company profile **Website** URL, or use Preview / Browse in Design.

Pair pages with [headers and footers](/docs/website-forms/headers-footers) and a [theme](/docs/website-forms/website-themes). Share the public address from [Public company site URL](/docs/website-forms/public-site-url).
