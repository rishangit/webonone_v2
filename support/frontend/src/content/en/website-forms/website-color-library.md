Super Admin maintains the **shared color library**. Company admins use it when adding colors to a theme.

## Super Admin: manage the library

1. Sign in as Super Admin.
2. Open **Website** → **Colors**.
3. Search, **Add color**, or delete colors from the library.

## Company admin: add colors to a theme

1. Open **Website** → **Themes** → your theme → **Colors**.
2. Choose **Add color**.
3. Pick **From library** to copy a shared color onto the theme, or **Custom** to name and pick a color for this theme only.

Super Admin custom colors on a theme are also saved to the shared library.

See [Website themes](website-themes.md).
