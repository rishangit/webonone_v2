title: "Point of Sale"
category: sales-billing
slug: pos
audience: owner
order: 1
summary: "Sell products, services, or spaces from Sales → POS and take cash, card, or other payment."
---

1. Log into the company.
2. Open **Sales → POS**.
3. Add catalog items to the bill.
4. Choose the customer if needed. The customer picker includes search and **Add** (new customer). On phone, those controls match the compact list toolbar — tap search or **+** to expand.
5. Take **cash**, **card**, or **other**, then complete the sale.

Items must exist in [Data](/docs/catalog/catalog-overview). Open [Sales history](/docs/sales-billing/sales-history) to reprint or review a bill. Company owners can see revenue and profit charts on [Analytics](/docs/app-preferences/analytics).
