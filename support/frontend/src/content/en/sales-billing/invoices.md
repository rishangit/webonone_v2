After a company is approved and activated, the platform creates a **subscription invoice** each month (LKR 3,000).

1. Open **Payment → Invoices**.
2. Open an invoice for amounts, period, and status.

Super Admins see invoices across companies. For status problems, see [invoice troubleshooting](/docs/glossary/troubleshooting-invoices).
