Not every company sells products, books services, and rents spaces. Turn on only what you need.

1. Open the company profile.
2. Find the **Data** card (products / services / spaces toggles).
3. Enable the types you use, then save.

Then build those lists under **Data** in the left menu. See [Catalog overview](/docs/catalog/catalog-overview).
