---
title: "Approve or reject companies"
category: companies
slug: approve-companies
audience: super_admin
order: 8
summary: "Super Admins review All Companies and approve pending requests or reject registrations."
---

1. Sign in and choose the **Super Admin** account.
2. Open **Companies → All Companies**.
3. Open a company whose status is **Pending**. Draft companies are still in setup — **Approve** stays unavailable until the owner requests approval.
4. **Approve** the pending request, or **Reject** if the registration should not go live.

The home dashboard pending list shows companies that have requested approval. Draft companies are not in that list.
