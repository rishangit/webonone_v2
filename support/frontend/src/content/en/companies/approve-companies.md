---
title: "Approve or reject companies"
category: companies
slug: approve-companies
audience: super_admin
order: 8
summary: "Super Admins review All Companies and approve or reject registrations."
---

1. Sign in and choose the **Super Admin** account.
2. Open **Companies → All Companies**.
3. Open a **pending** company.
4. **Approve** so the owner can log in, or **Reject** if the registration should not go live.

Owners cannot use Calendar, POS, or other company tools until the company is approved.
