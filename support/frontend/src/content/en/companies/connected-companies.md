As a **member**, you can connect to companies so you can book their offerings.

1. Stay on your Default User account.
2. Open **Companies → Connected Companies**.
3. Choose **Find companies**, search, and connect.
4. Open a connected company to browse **Our Products / Services / Spaces**. Only items the company has set to **Published** on the web appear here and in public marketplace search.

This does not make you a staff member or owner of that company.
