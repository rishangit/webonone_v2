Register as a **member** first, then create the company.

1. Sign in and choose your **Default User** account.
2. Open **Companies → My Companies**.
3. Choose **Add**.
4. Complete each wizard step (name and profile, contact, address, location if shown, and tags).
5. Submit. The company is created with status **Draft**.
6. **Choose account** opens, with that company selected. Choose **Continue** to work in the new company account. You can pick a different account instead.
7. Finish the company profile, then choose **Request approval** on the company page or from the **My Companies** menu. The status changes to **Pending**.

The company stays **Draft** until you request approval. After that it stays **Pending** until a Super Admin [approves](/docs/companies/approve-companies) it. You can still use the company account while it is Draft or Pending — see [pending approval](/docs/companies/pending-approval).
