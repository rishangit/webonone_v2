Register as a **member** first, then create the company.

1. Sign in and choose your **Default User** account.
2. Open **Companies → My Companies**.
3. Choose **Add**.
4. Complete each wizard step (name and profile, contact, address, location if shown, and tags).
5. Submit. The company is created with status **Pending**.

You cannot **Login** to that company until a Super Admin [approves](/docs/companies/approve-companies) it. Stay on your member account and wait — see [pending approval](/docs/companies/pending-approval).
