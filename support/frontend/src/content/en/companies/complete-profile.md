summary: "Fill profile, contact, address, map location, and tags from the company details page."
---

1. Log into the company.
2. Open the company from **Companies → My Companies** (or the company profile page).
3. Review each card: **profile**, **contact**, **address**, **location**, and **tags**.
4. Use **Edit** on a card (or the page Edit) to open the same wizard used at registration, starting on that step.
5. Save.

Keep the public name, phone, and address accurate — they appear on your catalog and public website.

The **Website** field on the profile card is generated from the company name (`https://{slug}.webonone.com`). It is not editable. See [Public company site URL](/docs/website-forms/public-site-url).
