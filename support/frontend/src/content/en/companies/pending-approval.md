title: "What happens while pending approval"
category: companies

summary: "A draft company stays on My Companies while you finish setup. After you request approval it stays pending until a Super Admin approves it."
---

A new company starts as **Draft**. You can keep editing the profile and use the company account. When setup is ready, choose **Request approval** on the company page or from **My Companies**. The status becomes **Pending** until a Super Admin approves it.

- You will see it on **My Companies** with a Draft or Pending status.
- Right after registration, **Choose account** opens with that company selected. **Continue** signs you into the company account.
- **Request approval** is available only while the company is Draft.
- If it is **Rejected**, you cannot use it as a company account. Contact the platform administrator if you need to resubmit.

You can also [log into the company](/docs/companies/log-into-company) later from My Companies.
