---
title: "What happens while pending approval"
category: companies
slug: pending-approval
audience: owner
order: 2
summary: "A pending company is visible under My Companies but you cannot log into it yet."
---

After you register a company it stays **Pending** until a Super Admin approves it.

- You will see it on **My Companies** with a pending status.
- **Login** stays unavailable until it is **Approved**.
- If it is **Rejected**, you cannot use it as a company account. Contact the platform administrator if you need to resubmit.

When it is approved, [log into the company](/docs/companies/log-into-company) from My Companies.
