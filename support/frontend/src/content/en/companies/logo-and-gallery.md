summary: "Add a company logo and gallery images with the Media picker."
---

1. Open the company profile.
2. On the **Overview** tab, open **Edit** on the **company profile** card. On the first wizard step, choose edit on the logo and pick an image from **Media** (or upload a new one). Crop 1:1 if asked, then save the wizard.
3. Open the **Gallery** tab to add more photos the same way.

Images are stored in Media, not copied as files on this help site. You can reuse the same library for catalog items and your website.
