---
title: "Log into an approved company"
category: companies
slug: log-into-company
audience: owner
order: 3
summary: "Open My Companies and choose Login on an approved company to work as the owner."
---

1. Sign in as your member user if you are not already.
2. Open **Companies → My Companies**.
3. Find the company with status **Approved**.
4. Choose **Login**.

The app switches to the **company owner** account. The left menu now includes Calendar, Identity, Data, Email, SMS, Sales, Payment, Design, and Settings.

To go back to your personal account, [switch account](/docs/getting-started/switch-account).
