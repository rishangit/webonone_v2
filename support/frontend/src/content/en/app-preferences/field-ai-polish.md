---

When your [AI API key](/docs/app-preferences/ai-api-key) is saved and working, a small **sparkle** icon appears inside eligible text boxes and text areas.

1. Type your text as usual.
2. Click the sparkle on the **right** of that field.
3. Wait for the icon to finish loading. Only **that** field is updated — spelling, grammar, and clarity. Other fields stay the same.

If the field is part of a form, the assistant uses the form labels and the values you already filled in as context, so names and descriptions stay consistent.

**No sparkle** on usernames, passwords, emails, phone numbers, search boxes, or other fixed identifiers. There is also no sparkle until AI is configured.

The in-app [chat assistant](/docs/app-preferences/ai-assistant) is separate — sparkle only rewrites the field you clicked.
