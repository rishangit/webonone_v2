After you sign in, **Dashboard** is the home page.

What you see depends on [role](/docs/getting-started/roles):

- Owners and staff see company sessions and events for today and upcoming days.
- Members see activity tied to connected companies and their own bookings.

Use the left menu to open [Analytics](/docs/app-preferences/analytics), Calendar, People, Data, and the rest of the app. Nothing on the Dashboard is required before you [register a company](/docs/companies/register-company).
