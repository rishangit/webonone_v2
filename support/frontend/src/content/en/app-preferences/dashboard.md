After you sign in, **Dashboard** is the home page.

What you see depends on [role](/docs/getting-started/roles):

- Owners and staff see company sessions and events for today and upcoming days.
- Members see activity tied to connected companies and their own bookings.
- Super Admins see a **Pending companies** list of companies whose owners requested approval. Draft companies stay off this list until then. About three companies show at once; scroll inside the card when there are more. Open a row to review that company. Approve or reject it from [All Companies](/docs/companies/approve-companies).
- Company owners see an **SMS Credits** card with remaining Text.lk credits when Text.lk is set up. Use refresh to update the balance. If Text.lk is not set up, the card is not shown. Super Admins see the same card on [SMS → Dashboard](/docs/communications/sms-overview). Set up Text.lk under [Devices and gateway settings](/docs/communications/sms-devices).

Use the left menu to open [Analytics](/docs/app-preferences/analytics), Calendar, People, Data, and the rest of the app. Nothing on the Dashboard is required before you [register a company](/docs/companies/register-company).
