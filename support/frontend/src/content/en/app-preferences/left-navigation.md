The left menu is grouped (Dashboard, Analytics, Calendar, Identity, Data, Email, SMS, Sales, Payment, Forms, Website, Companies, Settings). **Website** expands to Pages, Layouts, Headers, Footers, Presets, Data Sets, Themes, Media, and Settings. On the [mobile app](/docs/getting-started/mobile-app) the same groups open from the top-left menu. SMS includes **This device** on the phone only (the Android gateway).

Items appear only for the [account](/docs/getting-started/choose-account) you selected. Super Admin sees All Companies instead of a single company’s Calendar and POS. Super Admin also opens **Website** (Pages, Layouts, Headers, Footers, Presets, and Themes) to maintain the shared website library. Company admins add those items into their own site as a copy.

Click a row to open that page. Peer areas such as Email or Data load in the main content area of the app.
