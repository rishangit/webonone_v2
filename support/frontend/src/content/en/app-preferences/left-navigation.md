The left menu is grouped (Dashboard, Analytics, Calendar, Identity, Data, Email, SMS, Sales, Payment, Design, Companies, Settings). On the [mobile app](/docs/getting-started/mobile-app) the same groups open from the top-left menu. SMS includes **This device** on the phone only (the Android gateway).

Items appear only for the [account](/docs/getting-started/choose-account) you selected. Super Admin sees All Companies instead of a single company’s Calendar and POS.

Click a row to open that page. Peer areas such as Email or Data load in the main content area of the app.
