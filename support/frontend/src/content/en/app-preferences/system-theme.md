Company and platform admins can change accent colors used by the shell.

1. Open **Settings → System Theme**.
2. Create or edit a theme (name and colors).
3. Save. The palette applies according to how themes are assigned in your environment.

Most users only need [Appearance](/docs/app-preferences/appearance) (light/dark and Classic / High-tech).
