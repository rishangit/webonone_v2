summary: "Set light/dark, Classic or High-tech UI, and pagination vs on-scroll lists."
---

1. Open **Settings → Basic Settings**.
2. Open **Appearance**.
3. Choose:
   - **Light** or **Dark**
   - **Classic** or **High-tech** UI theme
   - List style: **pagination** (page numbers) or **on-scroll** (load more as you scroll)

These settings apply to list pages across the app.
