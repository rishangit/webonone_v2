---

1. Open **Settings → Basic Settings**.
2. Open **Appearance**.
3. Choose:
   - **Light** or **Dark**
   - **Classic** or **High-tech** UI theme
   - List style: **pagination** (page numbers) or **on-scroll** (load more as you scroll)
   - **Collection layout**: **List** (stacked rows), **Grid** (sortable columns), or **Card** (responsive tiles)

On any collection page you can also use the view buttons in the page header (next to search and filters) to switch list, grid, or card. The choice is saved to your account and applies across WebOnOne and connected apps.

Grid sorting applies to the items on the current page. Card view shows more items per row on wider screens.
