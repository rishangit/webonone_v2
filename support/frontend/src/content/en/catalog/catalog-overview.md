**Data** is your catalog library.

Typical order:

1. [Tags](/docs/catalog/tags) to classify items
2. [Units](/docs/catalog/units) of measure
3. [Attributes](/docs/catalog/attributes) (often with units)
4. [Products](/docs/catalog/products), [Services](/docs/catalog/services), and [Spaces](/docs/catalog/spaces)

Turn on which types you use on the [company Data card](/docs/companies/data-services). New tags and similar items may stay [unverified](/docs/catalog/unverified-items) until a Super Admin verifies them.
