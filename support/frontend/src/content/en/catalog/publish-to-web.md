As a **company admin**, each product, service, and space has a status. New items start as **Draft**.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | You are still setting the item up. |
| **Completed** | Ready to use inside the company. |
| **Published** | The completed item appears on your public website, marketplace search, company discovery previews, and connected-company browsing. |

The same status appears on the company catalog **list**, **grid**, and **card** views.

1. Open **Data → Products**, **Services**, or **Spaces** (company catalog).
2. Open the item detail page.
3. In **Status**, choose **Draft**, **Completed**, or **Published**.

Only **Published** items can appear in public search. Published items still must meet normal catalog rules (for example, verified custom items and approved company status).

## Share a published item

Published products, services, and spaces have **Share** in the actions menu on the catalog list and on the item page.

1. Open **Data → Products**, **Services**, or **Spaces**.
2. On a **Published** item, open the actions menu and choose **Share**.
3. Pick **WhatsApp**, **Facebook**, or **Share link**.
4. For **Share link**, enter an email address, a telephone number, or both, then choose **Send**.

WhatsApp and Facebook open that service with the public item link. The link includes the company id, so anyone who opens a product, service, or space can see which company it belongs to. Email and text messages use the **Catalog item share** template. Company admins can edit that wording under **Email → Templates** and **SMS → Templates**.
