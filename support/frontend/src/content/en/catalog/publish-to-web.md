summary: "Company admins choose which catalog items appear on the public website and marketplace."
---

As a **company admin**, new products, services, and spaces start as **Draft (internal only)**. Your team can use them in WebOnOne and Point of Sale; they are hidden from the public website, marketplace search, company discovery previews, and connected-company browsing until you publish them.

1. Open **Data → Products**, **Services**, or **Spaces** (company catalog).
2. Open the item detail page.
3. In **Website visibility**, choose **Publish to web → Published** (or leave **Draft** to keep it internal).

Published items still must meet normal catalog rules (for example, verified custom items and approved company status) before they appear in public search.
