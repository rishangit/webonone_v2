1. Open **Data → Spaces**.
2. Choose **Add space**. The wizard steps are **Basics**, **Tags**, **Attributes**, and **Summary**. You can also add a space from the Data library and edit it later on the company space page.
3. Link spaces from a [service](/docs/catalog/services) when the offering happens in that place.
4. Open the company space and choose **Attributes → Add attribute**. **Add** on a library-space row copies that attribute onto the company space. **Add new attribute** lists Data library attributes and attaches them to the linked library space; copy them onto the company space afterward if you want. **Add value** and **Create value** on the attribute page copy or create library values and link them to the company space.

Spaces can also appear in POS and on the public catalog when enabled. Adding a space from the library keeps the link to that Data library space.

Company admins set selling prices on the space **Prices & discounts** tab, the same way as services. The **Prices** card holds amounts and one active price. The **Discounts** card holds percentages off that active price, for **All time**, **Selected days**, or a **Selected range**, and for **Everyone** or **Selected users**. Overview shows the active price after the discount that applies right now.
