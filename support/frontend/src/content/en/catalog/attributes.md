---

1. Open **Data → Attributes**.
2. Add an attribute and attach a unit if it is measured.
3. Use attributes on product and service wizards so variants and filters stay consistent.
4. On a company product, service, or space, open **Attributes** and choose **Add attribute**. **Add** on a library-product row copies that attribute onto the company item. **Add new attribute** lists Data library attributes and attaches the ones you pick to the linked library product; copy them onto the company item afterward if you want. If the attribute does not exist yet, **Create attribute** in that picker. On the attribute page, **Add value** copies library values, and **Create value** writes a new value in the library and copies it onto the company item.
