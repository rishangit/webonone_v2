---

1. Open **Data → Products**.
2. Choose **Add** and complete the wizard (details, variants, stock).
3. Open a product to see the details page; use **Edit** on a card to jump to that wizard step.
4. Open a variant, then use the **three-dot menu** on each stock batch to **Edit** quantity, prices, dates, or supplier, or **Set as active**.

Enable products on the [company Data card](/docs/companies/data-services) if the menu is hidden. Sell products later in [POS](/docs/sales-billing/pos).
