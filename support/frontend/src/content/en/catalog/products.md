---
title: "Products, variants, and stock"
category: catalog
slug: products
audience: owner
order: 5
summary: "Add a company product from the Data library, then copy attributes and variants onto it."
---

1. Open **Data → Products**.
2. Choose **Add from library**. The company product keeps a link to that Data library product. Attributes and variants are not copied until you add them.
3. Open the product, then **Attributes**. Choose **Add attribute**. The left list is attributes on the linked library product. Use **Add** on a left row to copy that attribute onto the company product. The right list is company copies; use **Delete** to remove one.
4. To put more attributes on the library product, choose **Add new attribute** on the left. Select attributes from the Data library (or **Create attribute**) and confirm. They attach to the linked library product only. Copy them onto the company product afterward with **Add** on the left list if you want. On the attribute page, **Add value** copies library values that are not on the company item yet. **Create value** writes the value in the Data library and copies it onto the company product.
5. Open **Variants**. Choose **Add variant**. The left list is variants on the linked library product. Use **Add** on a left row to copy that variant onto the company product (it also copies the attributes and values it uses). The right list is company copies; use **Delete** to remove one (the default variant cannot be removed). **Add new variant** on the left creates the variant on the library product only. Copy it onto the company product afterward with **Add** if you want. Removing a variant removes attribute values that only that variant used. A value still used by another variant stays. You cannot remove an attribute or value while a variant still uses it.
6. Open a copied variant, then **Add** a stock batch. Use the **three-dot menu** on each batch to **Edit** quantity, prices, dates, or supplier, or **Set as active**. The product page **Pricing & stock** card shows the selected variant’s active stock price and quantities. Change the price on that stock batch, not on the product card.

Editing a copied value changes only your company copy. Enable products on the [company Data card](/docs/companies/data-services) if the menu is hidden. Sell products later in [POS](/docs/sales-billing/pos). See also [attributes](/docs/catalog/attributes).
