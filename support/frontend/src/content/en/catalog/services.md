summary: "Create services with time modes and optional spaces for Calendar."
---

1. Open **Data → Services**.
2. Add a service and set **time mode** (window vs duration) and any **spaces**.
3. Save. Calendar [events](/docs/calendar/create-event) pick from these services.

Details pages show reference counts (how many events or items use the service).
