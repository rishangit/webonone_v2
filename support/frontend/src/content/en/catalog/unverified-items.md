Tags and other platform library records may show **Unverified** until a Super Admin verifies them.

- You can still work on your company catalog.
- Super Admins verify items so they can be reused as trusted library records.
- Open the item details page to see verification status.

This is not the same as company [approval](/docs/companies/pending-approval).
