---

- **Token** — a queue number in a window-mode calendar session. Issued at the desk; called in order.
- **Analytics** — company charts and totals (revenue, profit, catalog, events). Separate from the Dashboard, which only shows today’s and upcoming events.
- **Profit** — completed-sale revenue minus product cost price (quantity × stock cost). Services and spaces have no stock cost.
- **Session** — one occurrence of an event on a given day (start, serve, end).
- **Connected company** — a business your **member** account linked to so you can browse and book, without becoming owner or staff.
- **Unverified** — a catalog library record (for example a tag) waiting for Super Admin verification.
- **Draft company** — just registered. The owner finishes setup, then requests approval. Choose account opens so you can use that company account while it is Draft.
- **Pending company** — the owner has requested approval and a Super Admin has not approved it yet. You can still use the company account while you wait.
- **Gateway** — a phone or provider that actually sends SMS.
