title: "Troubleshooting: invoice status"
category: glossary
slug: troubleshooting-invoices
audience: owner
order: 5
summary: "Open Payment → Invoices to see period and status; monthly invoices start after activation."
---

- Invoices appear after the company is **approved / activated**, not while pending.
- Amount is the platform subscription (LKR 3,000 / month) unless your administrator told you otherwise.
- Open the invoice row for dates and status. If none appear, wait until the next generation cycle or ask Super Admin to confirm Payment is running.
