---
title: "Troubleshooting: SMS gateway offline"
category: glossary
slug: troubleshooting-gateway
audience: owner
order: 4
summary: "OTP and SMS fail when the device is offline, unapproved, or stale."
---

- Confirm the [mobile app](/docs/communications/mobile-gateway) is installed, signed in, and the phone is online.
- Open **SMS → Devices** and **approve** the device.
- Keep the gateway app in the foreground or allowed to run in the background as your device requires.
- Check SMS **Queue** and **History** for error text.
- Password-reset OTP can also use email if SMS is down — try [email](/docs/getting-started/reset-password).
