summary: "If the login screen stays blank or you return to login, check account, password, and company status."
---

- Confirm you are opening the **app** URL, not only this help site.
- Use [Forgot password](/docs/getting-started/reset-password) if the password fails.
- If Google Sign-In is missing, use email and password.
- If you expected a company menu, the company may still be [pending](/docs/companies/pending-approval).
- After login, [choose the correct account](/docs/getting-started/choose-account).
