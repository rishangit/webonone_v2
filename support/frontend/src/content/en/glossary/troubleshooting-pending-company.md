---
title: "Troubleshooting: pending company"
category: glossary
slug: troubleshooting-pending-company
audience: owner
order: 3
summary: "After registration, Choose account opens so you can use the company while it is pending."
---

- Check **My Companies** for status **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**.
- Pending: **Choose account** opens after registration so you can continue as that company. Approval still happens separately — see [approval](/docs/companies/approve-companies).
- Rejected: contact the platform administrator; you cannot log into that company.
- Approved but no Login: refresh the list, then try Login again.
