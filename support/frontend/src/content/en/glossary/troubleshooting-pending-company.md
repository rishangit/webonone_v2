title: "Troubleshooting: pending company"
category: glossary
slug: troubleshooting-pending-company
audience: owner
order: 3
summary: "After registration the company is Draft. Choose account opens so you can use it, then request approval when setup is done."
---

- Check **My Companies** for status **Draft**, **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**.
- Draft: finish the profile, then choose **Request approval**. **Choose account** opens after registration so you can continue as that company.
- Pending: a Super Admin still needs to [approve](/docs/companies/approve-companies) the request. You can keep using the company account.
- Rejected: contact the platform administrator; you cannot log into that company.
- Approved but no Login: refresh the list, then try Login again.
