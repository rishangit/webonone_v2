---
title: "Issue a queue token"
category: calendar
slug: queue-token
audience: staff
order: 5
summary: "In a window-mode session, issue a token so the customer waits in line."
---

1. Open the running **window-mode** session.
2. Choose **Issue token** (or the equivalent action on the session screen).
3. Identify the customer if asked, then confirm.

The token appears in the queue. Use call next/previous to serve people in order. See the [glossary](/docs/glossary/glossary) for “token”.
