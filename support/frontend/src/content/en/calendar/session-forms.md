Some sessions include a **form**. Where you open it depends on how you signed in.

## Member login

Your session page has **no Overview or Workflow tabs** — it is one session detail page.

1. Open the session from your calendar.
2. Find the **Workflow** card. It lists **every** service step (staff and forms).
3. Choose **Fill form** (or **View form** after you submit) on the step that needs a form.
4. Fill the fields and submit.

## Company or staff login

1. Open the session and go to the **Workflow** tab.
2. During the session, open the form action when it appears.
3. Fill the fields and submit.

Forms are created under [Forms](/docs/website-forms/forms). You can fill them in the session without opening the full Design designer.
