title: "View the schedule"
category: calendar
slug: view-schedule
audience: owner
order: 1
summary: "Open Calendar → Schedule and switch day, week, or month."
---

1. Log into a company (or staff account).
2. Open **Calendar → Schedule**.
3. Switch **day**, **week**, or **month** as offered on the page.

Events you created under **Calendar → Events** appear on the schedule. Click an event or session to open details.
