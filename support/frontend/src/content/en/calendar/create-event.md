summary: "Add an event from Calendar → Events and pick a service and time mode."
---

1. Open **Calendar → Events**.
2. Choose **Add**.
3. Select the **service** (from your Data catalog) and fill the wizard steps for time, staff, and recurrence if needed.
4. Save.

Service **time modes** (window / duration) control how sessions and tokens work. Create services first under [Data → Services](/docs/catalog/services).
