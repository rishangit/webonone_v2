When you use a company **service**, **product**, or **space**, WebOnOne may ask you to **leave or update a review** by email, in-app notification, and (on the mobile app) push alert.

## From a completed session (services)

1. **Window / queue mode:** after you **complete your session workflow** (all steps done), you may receive a review request.
2. **Duration / appointment mode:** when the **session ends**, you may receive a review request.
3. Open the link in the email or tap the notification — it opens the **session** page with the review dialog.
4. Choose **1–5 stars**, add an optional comment, and submit.
5. You can **edit** your review anytime from the same **Your review** card.

## From a completed sale (products and spaces)

After a **completed POS sale** that includes products or spaces linked to your account, you may receive a review request for each catalog item. The link opens the **connected company catalog** item so you can rate it.

## From company catalog

On **Settings → Connected companies** (or **Data → company catalog** for members), open a **product**, **service**, or **space** detail page. Use **Write a review** or **Edit review** in the **Your review** section.

The mobile app follows the same flow on session and catalog detail screens.

## One review per item

You have **one review per catalog item** (per company product, service, or space) for your account. If you book again or buy again, you can **update** that same review — you do not create a second one.

## Company email templates

Companies can customize the **Catalog review request** email in the **Email** service under **Templates** (company-scoped copy of the platform default).

## Who does not get auto-prompts on session desk

Company staff operating the session queue are not prompted to rate from the staff session view. Customers receive the review requests instead.
