1. Open **Calendar → Events** and click the event.
2. Review times, staff, and recurrence (weekly, biweekly, monthly).
3. Use **Edit** to change the wizard step you need, then save.

Staff working days on the event control which days sessions can run.

## Expand a Specific-time series

When a Specific-time (window) event has an Until date, open **Upcoming sessions** and choose **Add** → **Expand series**.

1. **From** defaults to **today**. Change it if you need sessions to start on another day (including a past day in a gap).
2. **Until** is the new series end. It must be after the current Until.
3. Save.

Days between the **current Until** and **From** stay empty (service not held). Existing session days are not duplicated.

To fill part of a skipped gap later, open Expand series again and set **From** to an earlier date inside that gap.
