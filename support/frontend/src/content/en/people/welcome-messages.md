When you add a user, WebOnOne can send a welcome **email** and/or **SMS** if:

- Email templates and a provider are configured under [Email](/docs/communications/email-templates)
- SMS templates and a [device or gateway](/docs/communications/sms-devices) are ready

If delivery fails, the user is still added. Check Email/SMS history and gateway status rather than adding the person again.
