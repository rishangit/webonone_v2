---
title: "Verify email or phone"
category: people
slug: verify-email-phone
audience: all
order: 3
summary: "Request an OTP from your profile and enter the code to verify contact details."
---

1. Open **Profile**.
2. On email or phone, choose **Verify** if it is not verified yet.
3. Enter the OTP sent to that address or number.
4. Confirm.

If SMS does not arrive, see [SMS gateway troubleshooting](/docs/glossary/troubleshooting-gateway).
