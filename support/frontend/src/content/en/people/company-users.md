title: "Manage company users"
category: people
slug: company-users
audience: owner
order: 4
summary: "Open Identity → Users to add an existing person or register a new customer."
---

1. Log into the company.
2. Open **Identity → Users**.
3. Choose **Add**.
4. Pick an existing Identity user, or register a new person with name and contact.
5. Save. Optional welcome [email / SMS](/docs/people/welcome-messages) may send if templates and gateways are set up.

Open a user to see details and [history](/docs/people/user-history).
