---

1. Click your avatar in the header and choose **Profile** (or open Profile from the menu).
2. Review cards: account, name, contact, address.
3. Use **Edit** to change a section, then save.

Email and phone can be [verified with OTP](/docs/people/verify-email-phone). Photo is covered in [Profile photo](/docs/people/profile-photo).
