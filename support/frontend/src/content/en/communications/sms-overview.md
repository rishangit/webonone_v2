Open **SMS** in the left menu. Sub-pages typically include **Dashboard**, **Send**, **Devices**, **Queue**, **History**, and **Templates**.

Delivery uses either a registered phone ([mobile gateway](/docs/communications/mobile-gateway)) or a configured SMS provider (Text.lk).

On **SMS → Dashboard**, company owners and Super Admins see an **SMS Credits** card when Text.lk is the gateway for their scope. It shows remaining credits from Text.lk (with refresh). If Text.lk is not set up, the card links to [Devices and gateway settings](/docs/communications/sms-devices).
