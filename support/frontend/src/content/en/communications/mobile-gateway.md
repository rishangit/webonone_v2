---
title: "Install the mobile app as an SMS gateway"
category: communications
slug: mobile-gateway
audience: owner
order: 11
summary: "In the WebOnOne mobile app, open SMS → This device, sign in as owner or Super Admin, and register the phone."
---

1. Install the **WebOnOne** mobile app (Android gateway uses the device SIM). See [Use the WebOnOne mobile app](/docs/getting-started/mobile-app).
2. Sign in with the same account you use on the web.
3. [Choose the company account](/docs/getting-started/choose-account) (Super Admin or company owner — members do not see SMS).
4. Open the left menu → **SMS** → **This device** and register this phone.
5. On the web or in the app, [approve the device](/docs/communications/sms-devices) if asked.

Keep the app running and the phone online when you expect SMS to send.
