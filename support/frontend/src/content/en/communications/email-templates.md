1. Open **Email → Templates**.
2. Add a template, edit versions, and preview.
3. Use **Test email** from Email when you need a sample send.

## Activate or deactivate notifications

Company owners see platform **default** templates (welcome, sessions, appointments, and similar) plus any company copies.

- Open the row menu on a template and choose **Activate** or **Deactivate**.
- Deactivating a default creates a company copy marked inactive; the platform default is unchanged.
- While a template is **inactive** for your company, automated sends for that slug are **not delivered**. They appear in **Email → History** with status **Skipped** (template inactive).
- Manual **Test email** / **Send** while inactive returns an error so you know the template is off.

Welcome templates can still be provisioned when you [add a user](/docs/people/welcome-messages); turn them off here if you do not want welcome email.
