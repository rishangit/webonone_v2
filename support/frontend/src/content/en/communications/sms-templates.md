1. Open **SMS → Templates**.
2. Customize message text and preview placeholders where supported.

## Activate or deactivate notifications

Company owners see platform **default** templates (welcome, sessions, appointments, and similar) plus any company copies.

- Open the row menu on a template and choose **Activate** or **Deactivate**.
- Deactivating a default creates a company copy marked inactive.
- While a template is **inactive** for your company, automated SMS for that slug are **not sent**. They appear in **SMS → History** with status **Skipped** (template inactive).
- Manual test sends while inactive return an error so you know the template is off.
